From learning to the cell

Memory is biology

Every review in MemSurf is a bet on a biological event: an action potential crossing a synapse, and the synapse getting a little stronger for it. Follow the spikes along the axons. The strand on the right is where the instructions for all of it live.

Bioinformatics · individual project · 2022● spike at terminal

Autism genes: from a database to a network

Starting from the SFARI list of autism-linked genes, I asked three questions with Python and a few web tools: what is the literature saying about these genes, what do they do, and do their proteins work together? The strongest-evidence genes interact three times more than chance and split into two groups: the machinery of the synapse, and the machinery that switches genes on and off in a developing brain.

Data science · group project

World DataSet: what the numbers said

  • Parents' schooling dominates. r = 0.86 with the next generation's years in school; each extra year a parent had is worth about 0.67 years for the child.
  • Eight variables explain 83 %. Political stability, income and education spending all help; the fit held (R² 0.80–0.83) for every parent–child pairing.
  • Heat is a decoy. Hotter countries school their children less (r = −0.58), but split by income group the effect mostly disappears: geography standing in for wealth.
  • Scale before PCA. Unscaled, the first component was just "income in dollars"; standardised, three axes carry 85 % of the variation.
